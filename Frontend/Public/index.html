GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Who can use TradeCircle?

Businesses can create an account to browse wholesale products, place orders, and join eligible group deals.

Can a supplier also buy?

Yes. Approved suppliers can use buying and selling modes from one account.

How do group deals work?

Buyers contribute quantities toward a shared target. Each deal shows its progress, price, and closing time.

How does supplier verification work?

Businesses apply through their account. An administrator reviews the application before selling tools are enabled.
